GramSet — Privacy Policy

Developer: Marulas Studio · Effective 27 October 2026 · Android app “GramSet: Calories & Workouts” (package com.marulas.gramset)

In short: GramSet has no account, no server, no ads and no analytics or tracking SDK. Your meals, workouts, weight and every other record stay only on your own phone. If anything leaves your phone, you started it yourself: sharing a backup, looking up a barcode, sending a share card or writing feedback.

1. What is stored, and where

Everything you enter is kept in the app’s private storage on your phone, which other apps cannot read:

  • Foods, meal entries, recipes, foods you dislike and your nutrition targets
  • Weight, water, steps, sleep and body measurements
  • Routines, workout sessions, sets and templates
  • Progress photos (a copy of the picture you pick; your gallery is not touched)
  • Preferences such as reminders, theme and language
  • If the app crashes, a technical crash report (time, app version, Android version, device model and the stack trace). It contains none of your meals, weight or other personal data, and it leaves the phone only if you tap “Share”.

Android’s own cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are turned off for GramSet. Your data moves between phones only through a backup you make yourself.

3. Health Connect

Connecting Health Connect is optional and is turned on in Settings → Health Connect. If you grant permission, GramSet does the following:

  • Read (a separate permission per type): steps, weight and active calories; only the last 30 days and only while the app is open. Values read are shown on the Body screen, stored in the app’s own records and included in backups you make. A value you enter by hand always takes priority. Active calories are display-only; your calorie targets are not affected.
  • Write (optional, each switched on separately): workouts you finish (name, start and end time) and weight you enter by hand.

Data read from Health Connect stays only on this phone. It is never sent to a server, never used for advertising, analytics or any other purpose, and never shared with third parties. GramSet’s use and transfer of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can revoke permissions at any time per type in Settings → Health Connect, or all at once with “Disconnect”; values already read stay in the app and can be deleted one by one.

5. Exporting and deleting your data

  • Export: Settings → Data → backup (.zip with readable JSON and photos) or CSV.
  • Delete one entry: “Delete” in the entry’s sheet. A deleted entry no longer appears on any screen or in any export.
  • Delete everything: Android Settings → Apps → GramSet → Storage → “Clear data”, or uninstall the app. Since none of your data is on a server, no further deletion request is needed. Backups you exported and the automatic backup folder are yours to delete.
  • Delete the crash report: Settings → Data → “Last crash report” → “Delete”.

6. Children

GramSet is not designed for children under 16 and does not knowingly collect data from children. Because no data ever reaches the developer, the app asks for no age or identity information.

7. Changes

If the app’s data flow changes (for example a new permission or a new external service), this page is updated, the effective date above changes, and the change is mentioned in the release notes on the store listing.
